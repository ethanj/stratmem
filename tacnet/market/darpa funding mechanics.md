# DARPA Funding Mechanics

==DARPA funds high-risk, high-reward national-security tech. It is a **grant agency, not an equity investor** — performers can and do raise outside capital alongside DARPA awards. This note covers how the funnel works, what the award types are, what's required for disclosure, and how outside funding stacks.==

## What DARPA is (and isn't)

- **Defense Advanced Research Projects Agency** under the DoD.
- ==Annual budget **~$4+ billion**, much of it extramural.==
- Focus: revolutionary impact (not incremental science). Programs are time-limited.
- Each program is run by a **Program Manager (PM)** with significant autonomy — PMs serve **3–5 years**, often pulled from industry or academia, and act as hands-on technical champions.
- Not peer-reviewed like NSF. **PM judgment dominates** the selection process.

## The funnel — how to get funded

### 1. Identify the opportunity
- DARPA releases **Broad Agency Announcements (BAAs)** or specific program solicitations.
- Posted on **SAM.gov**, **Grants.gov**, and **darpa.mil**.
- Topic areas: AI, microelectronics, biotech, cyber, aerospace, materials, quantum, autonomy, comms, EW.
- For TacNet: **DARPA-PS-26-09** (Strategic Technology Office) is the active solicitation.

### 2. White paper / concept paper (often required)
- Most BAAs encourage or require a short white paper or abstract first.
- Quick PM feedback gates whether you're invited to submit a full proposal.
- ==**This is where founder credibility matters most**== — PMs decide what to fast-track.

### 3. Full proposal
- Submitted via Grants.gov (for grants / cooperative agreements) or other vehicles.
- Reviewed against the **Heilmeier Catechism**:
  1. What are you trying to do?
  2. How is it done today, and what are the limits?
  3. What's new in your approach, and why will it succeed?
  4. Who cares? If you succeed, what difference does it make?
  5. What are the risks?
  6. How much will it cost?
  7. How long will it take?
  8. What are the mid-term and final exams to check progress?
- ==Aggressive, transition-oriented framing wins.== Pure science framing loses.

### 4. Award
- Multiple instrument types — see below.
- Funded performers operate on **milestone-based, performance-gated** schedules.
- PMs can terminate underperforming work fast — there's no entitlement to continuation.

## Award types

| Vehicle | Use case | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Contracts (FAR-based)** | Specific deliverables, applied work | Most common for engineering work |
| **Grants / Cooperative Agreements** | Research-heavy, often universities | Cooperative agreements give DARPA more involvement |
| **Other Transactions (OTs)** | Prototypes, non-traditional performers | Flexible, non-FAR, commercial-friendly terms; allow cost-sharing |
| **SBIR / STTR** | Small businesses (TacNet's lane) | Phase I → II → III pipeline |

### SBIR / STTR specifics (TacNet's pathway)

| Stage | Typical amount | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| **Phase I** | **~$250,000** (fixed) | **6 months** — feasibility |
| **Phase I TCSP extension** | up to **$50,000** | +6 months |
| **Phase II** | **~$1,800,000** base, up to **$2,000,000** | **24 months** (sometimes 36) — prototype |
| **Phase II Enhancement** | up to **$500,000** matching outside investment **1:1** | rolling |
| **Phase II Direct / Phase IIE** | additional **$0.5–1M** | for transition partners |
| **Phase III / POR** | **$10M+** sole-source DoD contracts | open-ended pathway |

==Phase I → Phase II transition rate: **~50%** of awarded programs.==
==Phase I selection rate (i.e., chance of winning Phase I from a strong proposal): historically **~12–15%**.==

## Outside funding — yes, you can take it

==**Performers are allowed and encouraged to raise outside capital alongside DARPA awards.**== The DARPA portion is a contract scoped to specific program goals — it is not an equity instrument and does not block parallel fundraising.

### What's required

- **Current & Pending Support disclosure** — proposals and awards must list all sources of funding, effort levels, and in-kind contributions for key personnel (PIs, co-PIs, covered individuals). Aligned with NSF/DoD standards.
- **Effort commitments must be feasible** — total person-months across funded projects can't exceed reasonable capacity.
- **Conflicts and overlap rules** — DARPA may restrict or require approval for closely overlapping work.
- **Export controls / classification / IP** — Bayh-Dole for universities, ITAR/EAR for export-controlled tech, security classifications where applicable.

### How outside funding stacks (TacNet relevance)

| Outside source | How it interacts with DARPA |
|---|---|
| **VC / angel equity** | Fully compatible. Disclosed in Current & Pending. Many DARPA-funded startups raise VC in parallel. |
| **Phase II Enhancement match** | DARPA matches outside investment **1:1 up to $500K**. Outside money becomes leveraged. |
| **Other federal grants (NSF, NIH, AFWERX, DIU)** | Allowed, must disclose, can't double-bill effort. |
| **Industry partnerships / sponsored research** | Common, especially for universities. Must disclose. |
| **Cost-share on OTs** | Some OT programs *expect* cost-share from the performer. |

### Typical outside-funding patterns

- **Academia**: PIs often have DARPA covering 20–50%+ of a lab's effort, with NSF / NIH / industry / foundation funding making up the rest.
- **Small businesses**: Many raise meaningful VC or angel rounds in parallel or just after a DARPA award. The award itself is the **validation signal** — it de-risks the technical thesis for follow-on investors.
- **Phase II → Series A**: A common pattern is to use the DARPA award to hit milestones that justify a much larger equity round at a higher valuation.

==**Pure DARPA-only funding is rare for sustained efforts.** Most performers blend DARPA contracts with other sponsors.==

## What this means for TacNet

1. **The pre-seed round is non-conflicting** — DARPA is a grant; VC equity is separate and additive. We disclose and proceed.
2. **Phase II Enhancement is leverage**: every $1 of outside investment can pull in up to another $1 of DARPA matching cash, capped at $500K.
3. **Distribution is solved structurally** — DoD is the customer, DARPA is the front door, PMs are the gatekeepers. No CAC, no marketing funnel; we sell to a single buyer with a single intake mechanism.
4. **The DARPA award is the validation signal** for follow-on Series A — defense-tech VCs explicitly look for DARPA traction as a quality filter.

## How TacNet wins a DARPA Phase I

1. **Solicitation fit** — DARPA-PS-26-09 (STO) maps directly onto our architecture.
2. **PM relationship** — our 2LT EW-officer credential is exactly the operator profile a PM in this space wants to see.
3. **Cornell co-PI** — STTR's institutional partner requirement is satisfied; signals research rigor.
4. **Heilmeier compliance** — the Shannon-limit math + the Mosaic Warfare alignment + the working iOS prototype answer all 8 questions cleanly.
5. **Working code on day one** — "we already built it" beats "we will build it" 9 times out of 10 in PM judgment.
