# DARPA University Partners — Top 10 + Notes

==DARPA does not publish an official "top 10" — this list is synthesized from NSF HERD DoD R&D rankings, UARC / FFRDC affiliations, and DARPA program participation history.==

## The consensus top 10

| # | University | DARPA-relevant edge |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Johns Hopkins University** | Hosts **JHU APL** (UARC). #1 in DoD R&D spend for 20+ years. C2, autonomy, EW. |
| 2 | **MIT** | Hosts **MIT Lincoln Laboratory** (FFRDC). Radar, comms, advanced sensors, AI. |
| 3 | **Carnegie Mellon (CMU)** | Hosts the **SEI** (FFRDC) and the **CTI**. AI, robotics, cyber, autonomy. DARPA Grand Challenge legacy. |
| 4 | **Georgia Tech** | Hosts **GTRI** (UARC-equivalent). EW, RF, sensors, comms — direct fit for TacNet. |
| 5 | **Stanford** | Original ARPANET node. AI, semiconductors, quantum, autonomy. |
| 6 | **Penn State** | Hosts **ARL Penn State** (UARC). Materials, undersea, Navy-focused. |
| 7 | **UT Austin** | Hosts **ARL:UT** (UARC). Acoustics, signal processing, comms. |
| 8 | **University of Washington** | Hosts **APL-UW** (UARC). Maritime, sensing, undersea. |
| 9 | **USC** | Hosts **USC ICT** (UARC). Human-machine teaming, AI, immersive simulation. |
| 10 | **Caltech** | Operates **JPL** with NASA. Quantum, autonomy, advanced computing. |

## Honorable mentions (frequent DARPA performers)
- **UC Berkeley** — RISC-V, OS, AI
- **UIUC** — networking, parallel computing, ag-tech
- **University of Michigan** — autonomous vehicles, robotics
- **Cornell** — semiconductors, materials, AI (==Yifu's MS partner — already TacNet's STTR co-PI==)
- **Princeton, Harvard** — AI policy + theory
- **Texas A&M, Purdue, Northeastern, NYU** — defense engineering

## Why this matters for [[tacnet homepage|TacNet]]
- ==STTR explicitly requires an academic research partner.== Cornell already fills that role.
- For Phase II expansion or specialized capability (RF/EW work, on-device ML acceleration), the natural cross-collaboration partners are:
  - **Georgia Tech (GTRI)** — RF/EW/comms expertise
  - **MIT Lincoln Lab** — tactical comms + edge AI
  - **CMU** — on-device AI + autonomy
  - **JHU APL** — C2 systems integration
- These four would also be the most credible names to cite in any DARPA Phase II proposal narrative.
