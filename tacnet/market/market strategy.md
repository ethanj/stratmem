# Market Strategy — B2G & The DARPA Path

==Primary customer: U.S. Department of Defense, via the Small Business Technology Transfer (STTR) program.==

## Target solicitation
- **DARPA-PS-26-09** — Strategic Technology Office (**STO**).
- Aligned with the DoD's shift toward **Mosaic Warfare**: from monolithic, expensive platforms → disaggregated, **attritable** (disposable) systems.

## Why STTR (not SBIR)
- STTR **requires** a formal research-institution partner.
- We already have a **research collaboration with Cornell University** — the partnership accelerates Phase I and Phase II eligibility.
- STTR also accepts dual-use technology, which gives us a civilian fallback runway.

## The funnel
```
Pre-seed (now)
   ↓
STTR Phase I  — feasibility, $0.05–$0.30M, 6–12 months
   ↓
STTR Phase II — prototype, $1.0–$2.0M, 24 months
   ↓
Phase III / Program of Record — sole-source DoD contracts
```

## Mission alignment
- DARPA STO funds **resilient C2** and **anti-A2/AD** technologies.
- TacNet maps directly: zero-cloud, low-RF-signature, software-defined, AI-augmented C2.
- Founder's [[founder profile|2LT National Guard]] credibility de-risks the "do these people understand the warfighter" question that kills most defense pitches.

## Strategic partnership — Cornell
- Formal R&D collab leveraging academic compute and graduate research.
- Co-PI structure satisfies STTR institutional requirements out of the box.
- Halo effect on hiring, IP licensing, and follow-on grant submissions.

## Dual-use potential (civilian markets)
- **First responders** — fire / SAR teams in canyons, basements, wilderness.
- **Mining** — underground operations with no signal.
- **Maritime** — offshore crews where satellite is lossy or jammed.
- **Disaster relief** — earthquake / hurricane zones with destroyed cell infrastructure.
- **Construction sites & large events** — cheap radio replacement with AI summarization.

## Customer journey
1. Win STTR Phase I → get into DoD vendor system (CAGE / DUNS / SAM.gov).
2. Field demos with one warfighter unit (likely MN Guard via founder's network).
3. Phase II → POR pipeline → land-and-expand into adjacent commands.
4. Civilian SKUs as side-revenue + brand defensibility.
