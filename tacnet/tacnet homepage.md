# TacNet Homepage

==TacNet is a decentralized, AI-native communication layer for the "Contested Edge"== — environments where satellite, LTE, and cloud are jammed or compromised. Software-first, hardware-agnostic, on-device intelligence.

- **One-liner**: every phone is an intelligent tile in a self-healing mesh that compresses voice into intent tokens so the squad stays connected at near-zero bitrate.
- **Founder**: [[founder profile|Yifu Zuo]] — 2LT MN National Guard + Cornell MS Business Analytics + Georgia Tech CS + UMN Applied Math.
- **Customer**: U.S. Department of Defense via [[market strategy|DARPA STTR]] (DARPA-PS-26-09, STO).
- **Stage**: BLE-mesh prototype on iOS (Cactus + Gemma 4 E4B). Pre-seed.
- **Code repo**: `/Users/yifuzuo/Desktop/yifu/startup/projects/voice-agents-hack/OLD CODE`
